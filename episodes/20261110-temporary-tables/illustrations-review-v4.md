# Illustrations review v4: final cut of #196 (25-final-v4.mp4)

[Plan as built](illustrations.md) · [Earlier review and plan v2](illustrations-review.md)

Independent art-director review of all 31 cards in `25-final-v4.mp4` (1920x1080, 2320.32 s). Times are FINAL video time (edited + 14 s), as `mm:ss` and seconds. Method: 9+ frames per card, 1 s timelines for the hero cards, a 390 px phone sheet, word timing from `src.words.json` through `01-edited.cuts.json`, numbers against `forge-data.md` and the local V6.1 brief text. Sound effects were not reviewed (frames only). Frames: `/private/tmp/claude-501/fable-review-196/`.

Phone rule used: in portrait a 1920 px frame is 390 pt wide, so text under about 40 px (8 pt) is not read, and under 32 px is decoration.

## 1. Overall verdict

- Score: 6.5 / 10. Clean, consistent, honest and well timed, but not "awesome" yet. The psql card (24), the ring (21) and the cliff (17) are close to the bar. The rest is tidy slides.
- Biggest problem 1, facts: card 16 shows two numbers that are not in the brief (0 idle: CPU 95.1 %, IO 1.65 %; the brief says 95.0 and 1.8) under a caption "Shares as stated in the brief". Card 21 labels "~114 messages" on an arc that covers about 40 % of the 4,096 slots. Card 19b says the opposite of what Nik says at that moment and gives no framing.
- Biggest problem 2, phone legibility: every secondary layer is 28-30 px (subtitles, axis ticks, captions, band chips, source lines, the Fig. 6.2b strip, psql at about 30 px, the brief paragraph, the wiki). On a phone the viewer gets the title and the big numbers only. The "more technical" part of the charts is the part that cannot be read.
- Biggest problem 3, rhythm: the first hero visual comes at 12:08. From 2:06 to 12:08 there are five small strips in ten minutes (gaps of 156 s and 106 s). Then long static holds: 08 (54 s, a list), 11 (28 s with no change), 19b (14 s of an empty chart), 23 (114 s, three events). The late screenshots (27, 28 shot 2, 30) are soft, dense or empty of meaning.
